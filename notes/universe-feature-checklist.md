# The Unmaking: universe feature checklist (Oct 11, ~1am)
Every feature in the simulation, how to see it, and what to watch for. Clip each one for polish notes.
Keys: **Insert** (full ult), **G** (sit in her universe), **B** (birth at mouse, during a cast or G), **N** (+60s cosmic time), **F10** (debug black holes), **F6** (+50 unmade: ascension tier), **V** (a giant at the mouse that goes supernova in ~3s; with F10 it collapses into a black hole instead).

## The cast
1. **Entrance:** glitch bars, stillness with the sigil, the crack running in and forking, the impact frame (BOOM), the ground breaking into slabs over the chasm. *(Watch: the blank frame at cast start.)*
2. **The crumble:** each enemy breaks apart head-first, pixel by pixel, rippling outward from her.
3. **Dust:** each mote is a real pixel of the enemy, tumbling as a solid chunk, then burning down into glowing dust.
4. **Gas colors from what they were made of:** pink hydrogen, teal oxygen, gold sulphur, blue reflection, violet (hers); dark pixels become soot (dark lanes). Rainbow slimes give rainbow gas.
5. **Star dust threads:** about 30% of each body streams into its star as bright threads; the star catches when its last thread lands.
6. **Stars colored by mass:** a lone enemy is a red dwarf, a clump a yellow-white sun, a big crowd a blue giant.
7. **The finale pulse:** a wave through this cast's newborn stars, then the newborns drift together and merge (accretion).
8. **Newborn halo:** this cast's stars keep a soft glow until the sky closes.

## The nebula (lasting gas)
9. **Haze persists:** some settled dust stays as lasting gas, and the horde's shape is written into the sky.
10. **Texture:** billowing clouds and long threads (8 procedural shapes), with dark soot lanes.
11. **Exposure curve:** dense gas glows rich but never whites out.
12. **Merging between casts:** crowded gas merges into bigger, softer clouds; old gas slowly spreads and thins.
13. **Her violet:** her ascension violet shows in the backdrop nebula structures (backdrop currently off for testing via _covers 0.99).

## Births (the peek, and G)
14. **Density-driven births:** the densest gas collapses on its own (each cast, and continuously in G).
15. **Bok globule:** a dark knot with a lit rim forms as the gas pulls in.
16. **Infall spiral:** comet-trailed motes winding inward, fast near the center (Kepler).
17. **Protostar:** a dim, flickering red warmth inside the knot.
18. **Jets (Herbig-Haro):** knots plus an aerosol spray plus a wide faint wind, from both poles; white-teal at the base, blue along the jet, pink shock tips; bow-shock arrowheads; an edge-on disk dark lane; a pale hourglass; the far jet dimmer and redder; an S-shaped trail from precession.
19. **The catch:** a flare, a ring of light sweeping out through the gas, the lamp glow in the star's color, the bubble pushing gas back.
20. **The garden trim:** the star eats ~30% of its gas, ~12% stays as its pocket, and the rest is blown out along the jets as long pink streaks (birth scars).

## Aging and death
21. **Red giants:** mid-mass stars in their last 30% of life swell and turn orange-red.
22. **Red supergiants:** heavy stars do the same, bigger.
23. **Planetary nebula:** a mid-mass star throbs three times, then puffs off a lumpy red-outside, teal-inside ring and leaves a **white dwarf** (tiny white-blue). The ring becomes haze.
24. **Supernova:** a giant's core flickers white-blue faster and faster; the impact frame hits; a flash; a shell of ~90 teal/gold/red filaments tears out and slows; it settles into a ring of thread-gas (the remnant). Visible as a ring/lattice of threads in the nebula afterward.
25. **Neutron star / pulsar:** a tiny pale-blue core with two beams sweeping round like a lighthouse.
26. **Collapse to a black hole** (heaviest stars, tier 4+ or F10): the gather (streaks rushing in, the star swells then is crushed), the impact frame, the shockwave, then the hole.

## Black holes (polish next)
27. The disk (streaks orbiting, faster inside), the lensed ring, the event horizon, the photon ring, the bright approaching side. Holes pull newborn stars in and swallow them.

## Sandbox / time
28. **Cosmic time between casts:** births scale with time away and the amount of gas; stars age by time away.
29. **G mode:** the world freezes, her sky lives in real time (aging, births, deaths); G to leave.
30. **Recycling:** rings and remnants become gas, which becomes new stars.
