# Garden visual checklist
Each system: what you should see in game, then the improvement in [brackets]. Tick it when it looks right. (Oct 11, ~7am)

## Birth and life
- [ ] **Stardust:** enemies burn away pixel by pixel; their dust drifts like smoke or streams in as bright threads. [Fewer motes, the same look: performance.]
- [ ] **Nebula:** soft textured clouds in your enemies' colors, with dark dust lanes. [Varied ring shapes, deeper dark lanes.]
- [ ] **Births:** a dark knot gathers, matter spirals in, jets fire from the poles, the star ignites. [Jets less white and dominant.]
- [ ] **Stars:** colors by mass (red dwarf → yellow → blue giant), aging ones swell orange-red. [Tinted dwarfs and giants, visible surface spots on giants.]
- [ ] **Planetary nebula:** a dying star throbs and puffs off a pink-and-teal ring around a white dwarf. [Butterfly and hourglass shapes.]
- [ ] **Supernova:** a flash, filaments flying outward, a pulsar's sweeping beams left behind. [A flash just before, cooling colors, a light echo, a lasting filament shell.]

## Black holes
- [ ] **Formation:** rings of light falling inward, the star crushed, the impact frame, gamma-ray beams from the poles, a ripple, then the photon ring igniting and the disk building. [Smoother timing.]
- [ ] **The look:** a black shadow, a bright brim arcing over the top, a turbulent disk crossing in front, a thin warm photon ring, the light around it bent. [More uniform disk, click-to-zoom.]
- [ ] **Feeding:** nearby gas spirals in as curved streams and melts into the disk, which brightens while it eats. [Smoother streams.]
- [ ] **Outflow:** soft blue-white gas thrown from the poles, cooling to violet far away. [Smoother, real jet shape.]
- [ ] **Mergers:** two holes spiral faster and faster, ripples roll out, they merge, and the new horizon wobbles like a bell. [More warp and warble.]

## The galaxy and the aeon
- [ ] **Supermassive:** a huge hole that the whole sky slowly turns around. [Clearer spiral arms.]
- [ ] **Star tearing:** every so often, a nearby star is flashed and stretched into an arc around it. [Make it readable.]
- [ ] **Quasar:** the disk blazes, jets cross the whole screen, gas blows outward, everything turns blue-white. [The Fermi bubbles.]
- [ ] **Twilight:** no new stars, everything warm and amber, gas dimming, old stars dying. [Stars visibly reddening.]
- [ ] **Crunch:** everything spirals in, faster and faster, the sky shifting blue and brightening, the backdrop fading, the horizon swelling. [The scripted cinematic.]
- [ ] **Void:** full black, HUD and witch included, for about a second. [Confirm it shows.]
- [ ] **Big Bang:** a white flash, a blinding core, a shell of light racing outward, soft white-hot gas blooming outward in the new colors, a mottled orange afterglow cooling. [The matter ledger: release exactly what the crunch took.]
- [ ] **Dawn:** the first stars ignite in the clumps, and the background stars fade back in. [The dark ages, then glowing bubbles around the first stars.]
